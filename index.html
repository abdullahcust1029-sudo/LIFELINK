About LifeLink

LifeLink is a community-focused NGO website concept designed to encourage voluntary blood donation and connect donors with people who need blood.

Our mission is to make blood donation awareness easier, promote kindness, and help people understand the importance of becoming a regular blood donor.

  • Promote voluntary blood donation
  • Encourage community participation
  • Connect donors with blood requests
  • Spread awareness about safe donation
